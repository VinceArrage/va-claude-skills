A task from Ready to Done

Every agent and skill call in the dev-loop, in order, from a GitHub issue to a merged and closed task.

Read left to right by stage and top to bottom by who acts. The numbers give the order. Filled dark-red nodes are where the loop stops and waits for you. Nothing else in the diagram needs you at the screen. A small task stops exactly five times: intake, the approach brief, the UI walk, the PR open, and the delivery package.

Stop: you act Gate that auto-passes (or stops) Sigmund, bookkeeping and sequencing Sev hat-swap, in session Clean-room dispatch, fresh context Skill, doc, or tool call

The main pipeline

The dashed return arrows are the two loops that run without you. Review findings turn into Sev fixes and a re-verify until Ellie has no notes left. CI results and Copilot comments get triaged at every wake-up until the PR is clean. The adversarial cases are written from the brief's risk surface without sight of Sev's verification plan. Sigmund merges the two into the walk sheet you drive, and Ellie then checks that the walk covered them.

The Dev Review track

When the hand-over is someone else's finished PR on one of your repos, the task enters at Verification and the shape changes. Fixes go back to the author rather than to Sev, and the gates are the review set: comments, merge, notify.
